Privacy policy

Under articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) · Last updated: 25 October 2026

Data controller

Lucia Murgolo, via Girolamo Gigli 17, 53100 Siena, Italy. Email: info@lafinestrasulverde.it.

What data we collect

  • Booking: full name, email, phone (optional), dates of stay, number of guests, amounts paid.
  • Payment: card details are entered directly on Stripe's secure page; we never see or store them. We only receive the payment result and, when you choose pay on arrival, a reference to the stored card.
  • On arrival: ID document details for each guest, as required by law.
  • Browsing: the website uses no profiling cookies or third-party analytics. Dates searched in the calendar are recorded anonymously and in aggregate, with no personal data, to estimate demand. Our hosting provider keeps access logs, including IP addresses, for technical and security purposes.

Why we process it and on what legal basis

  • To manage your booking, stay, payments and communications: performance of a contract (art. 6.1.b GDPR).
  • To register guests with the police (art. 109 TULPS), handle the city tax and statistical reporting, and meet tax and accounting obligations: legal obligation (art. 6.1.c).
  • To keep the website secure, prevent fraud and protect our rights, e.g. for damage or unpaid amounts: legitimate interest (art. 6.1.f).

Who we share it with

With the providers that help us run the service, appointed as processors or acting as independent controllers:

  • Stripe: payments.
  • Netlify: website hosting and booking storage.
  • Resend: confirmation emails.
  • Airbnb and Booking.com: only the booked dates, with no guest data, to prevent double bookings.

We also share data with public authorities where required by law: police, Municipality and tax authorities. Some providers are based in the United States: transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Booking and payment data are kept for 10 years, as required by tax law. Police registrations are not kept beyond the legal term. Data from bookings that were not completed is deleted within 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its processing, or receive it in a portable format, within the limits of legal obligations, by writing to info@lafinestrasulverde.it. You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (garanteprivacy.it).
