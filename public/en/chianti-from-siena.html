The Chianti in a day,
from Siena

The Chianti starts just outside Siena. By car you can do a one-day loop through villages, castles and vineyards and be back for dinner. Free parking near the house makes an early start easy.

A possible route

  • Castellina in Chianti: head north on the Chiantigiana road (SR222). The village has a fortress and the atmospheric via delle Volte.
  • Radda in Chianti: small and charming, with fine views over the vineyards.
  • Gaiole in Chianti and Brolio Castle: Brolio is the historic Ricasoli estate, one of Italy's oldest wineries. Gaiole is also home to L'Eroica, the vintage bike ride in October.
  • Panzano in Chianti: known for its butcher shops and the view over the Conca d'Oro.
  • Greve in Chianti: Piazza Matteotti, with its arcades and wine shops, about 42 km from the apartment. From here, head back to Siena.

Practical tips

  • Book tastings: many wineries take visitors by appointment only, especially at weekends.
  • The driver doesn't drink: checks are frequent. Alternatively, there are guided tours with a driver departing from Siena.
  • The best season is April to October. The grape harvest is in September and October.
  • By bike: the Chianti is a classic cycling destination, and Siena is the home of the Strade Bianche.
