A graduation in Siena,
to celebrate together

Siena is a university city: with the University of Siena and the University for Foreigners, families and friends arrive all year round for graduation sessions. Bookings are often last-minute, once the date is confirmed, and places in the centre fill up fast.

Why La Finestra sul Verde

  • Up to 4 people: a double bedroom and a double sofa bed, ideal for parents and siblings.
  • One-night minimum stay: arrive the evening before and leave after the celebration.
  • Free parking nearby, handy if you're driving in from out of town.
  • A 15-minute walk from Piazza del Campo and the university buildings in the historic centre.
  • A terrace for a family toast, before or after the celebration dinner.

Practical tips

  • Book as soon as you have the date: demand rises in graduation weeks.
  • Check the venue for the ceremony: faculties are spread across the city.
  • Book the restaurant well ahead: on graduation days places in the centre fill up.
  • Allow plenty of time: sessions can last several hours, with many candidates.
