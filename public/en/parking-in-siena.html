Where to park in Siena,
stress-free

In Siena you leave the car outside the walls: almost the whole historic centre is a limited traffic zone (ZTL), and cameras at the entry points fine any car without a permit automatically.

What the lines mean

  • White lines: free parking, unless signs say otherwise.
  • Blue lines: paid, by meter or app.
  • Yellow lines: reserved for residents, disabled badges or loading. No parking.

The advantage of La Finestra sul Verde

Many guests mention it in their reviews: the streets near the apartment have free white-line parking, just outside Porta Pispini. Leave the car and walk into the centre, with no ZTL and no meters.

Practical tips

  • Always read the signs: some streets have night-time cleaning bans or time limits.
  • Heavy luggage? Stop briefly near the entrance to unload, then move the car.
  • Multi-storey car parks: the city has paid car parks, some linked to the centre by escalators. Handy if you're arriving from another direction.
  • During the Palio and the Strade Bianche the streets fill up early and some roads close: arrive in good time.
