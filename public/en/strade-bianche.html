The Strade Bianche,
close to the finish

Every year in early March, Siena hosts the Strade Bianche: the professional cycling classic raced on the gravel roads of the Sienese hills, finishing in Piazza del Campo after a final climb through the historic centre.

On race weekend the Gran Fondo Strade Bianche is also held on the same gravel roads, open to amateur cyclists.

Why stay at La Finestra sul Verde

  • 200 m from Porta Pispini and about a 15-minute walk from Piazza del Campo: watch the finish and walk back home.
  • Free parking in the nearby streets, handy if you're driving in with your bike.
  • Up to 4 guests, with an equipped kitchen for breakfast before the start.
  • One-night minimum stay: ideal even just for race weekend.

Practical tips

  • Book early: on race weekend the city fills up with fans and teams.
  • Check the official dates and programme on the organisers' website: they change every year.
  • To see the finish, head into the centre early: the streets of the final kilometre fill up quickly.
  • In March days can be cool: bring something warm for the evening.
