2. Booking

A booking made on this website is confirmed once the full amount or the deposit, as chosen by the guest, has been paid online. The guest then immediately receives a confirmation email with a summary of the stay. The person booking declares that they are of age and act on behalf of the other guests listed.

3. Prices

Prices are in euros per night and vary by period. The total shown before payment includes the stay, final cleaning, linen and the Siena city tax, which the host pays to the Municipality. There are no other mandatory charges.

4. Payment

Online payments are processed by Stripe: card details never pass through or are stored on our website. Two options are available:

  • Full online payment: the full amount is paid immediately, with the discount shown at booking.
  • Pay on arrival: a deposit (percentage shown at booking) is paid online and the balance is paid at check-in, in cash or by card. The card used for the deposit is stored by Stripe as a guarantee. By booking, the guest authorises the host to charge that card, after notice by email, for: any balance not paid on arrival, the penalties in section 5 and the cost of any confirmed damage to the apartment.

5. Cancellation and no-show

  • Up to 7 days before arrival: free cancellation with a full refund.
  • Within 7 days of arrival: amounts already paid are non-refundable. With pay on arrival, the deposit is retained.
  • No-show without notice: the host may charge the full price of the stay.
  • Early departure: unused nights are not refunded.

To cancel, simply reply to the confirmation email. If the host has to cancel for reasons not attributable to the guest, all amounts paid will be refunded in full.

6. Arrival, departure and documents

Check-in from 4 pm to 10 pm; check-out by 10 am. Late arrivals must be agreed in advance. On arrival every guest must show a valid ID: Italian law (art. 109 TULPS) requires the host to register all guests with the police within 24 hours.

7. House rules

  • The number of guests may not exceed the number stated in the booking.
  • Smoking indoors is not allowed. No parties or events.
  • Pets: not allowed.
  • Please respect quiet hours from 11 pm to 7 am.

8. Liability

Guests are responsible for any damage caused to the apartment and its furnishings during their stay. The host is not liable for valuables left unattended.

9. Governing law and complaints

Italian law applies. For consumers, the court of their place of residence has jurisdiction. Statutory consumer rights are not affected. The 14-day right of withdrawal does not apply to accommodation services for specific dates (art. 59(n) Italian Consumer Code). Complaints: oli.giuann@gmail.com.
