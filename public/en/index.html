The apartment

The rooftops of Siena,
green on the horizon

La Finestra sul Verde is a bright, welcoming apartment for experiencing Siena at a slower pace. The terrace is the heart of the home: from here the view sweeps across the city's rooftops to the mountains beyond. It's the perfect spot for breakfast in the morning and a glass of wine at sunset.

The rooms are simple and thoughtfully kept. There's a bedroom, a living room to unwind in after a day exploring the contrade and museums, and a kitchen with everything you need to feel at home. Air conditioning keeps you comfortable even on the hottest summer days.
