The apartment

A green valley,
Siena close by

La Finestra sul Verde is a welcoming apartment, lovingly furnished and full of detail, for experiencing Siena at a slower pace.

From the windows the view sweeps across a valley of olive groves and gardens, with the viaduct and the city walls on one side and the hills rolling to Mount Amiata on the other. The terrace with its pergola is the perfect spot for breakfast in the morning and a glass of wine at sunset.

The historic centre, with its medieval palaces, contrade and Piazza del Campo, is within walking distance.

FAQ

Your questions,
our answers

How many people can stay?

Up to 4: two in the double bedroom and two on the double sofa bed in the living room. Prices are for 2 guests; from the third guest a small nightly supplement applies, shown in the quote.

How does payment work?

You can pay for the whole stay online now, or pay a deposit and settle the balance on arrival, in cash or by card. Payments are handled securely by Stripe.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes, free of charge up to 7 days before arrival, with a full refund. All the details are in the terms and conditions.

What are check‑in and check‑out times?

Check‑in is from 4 pm to 10 pm, check‑out by 10 am. If you're arriving later, just message us and we'll work something out.

Is the city tax included?

Yes, the total shown before payment already includes the Siena city tax.

Is there parking?

There is free on-street parking (white lines) in the nearby streets, as many of our guests mention: not a given in Siena.

Are pets allowed?

We're sorry, pets are not allowed.

How do I get to the centre?

On foot: Piazza del Campo is about 1.3 km away. Detailed directions come with your booking confirmation.
